# Story continuity pass

## Canon

The Redactor drains registered hero powers through district relays. The Fortress shield protects the heroes inside; outside it, their known signatures make them vulnerable. Volt arrives with unregistered lightning. Patch equips him with remote support channels and an extraction beacon.

Each operation disables three district relays, opens the tower shield, gets past Gravemark and reaches the Redactor's control room. Hero upgrades are channelled through Volt's suit. Extraction burns out those temporary links; permanent Fortress improvements survive. Defeat means rescue and regrouping, not unexplained resurrection. Relay crews restore interrupted routes between attempts.

A victory shuts down the main drain and returns stolen power. The Redactor escapes through his backup network. Later operations target his renewed infrastructure. The victory matters without pretending the antagonist was permanently killed.

## Delivery and knowledge

- The opening establishes the shield, registered signatures, Volt's opportunity and the extraction beacon. First deployment explains the full route and upgrade loop. Existing saves receive that briefing without resetting their progress.
- The route provides short mission updates before Gravemark and the tower. Victory has a team exchange before results. Defeat results explicitly state that Patch extracted Volt.
- Comms conversations continue relationships from the opening instead of introducing strangers again. Fortress conversations no longer contradict Halo accepting Volt, and Echo's banter no longer claims she deliberately withheld a life-saving warning.
- Conversations only count as heard after their final page. Abandoning one does not unlock follow-up references. Older saves retain their progress.
- Later Halo/Redactor revelations suppress obsolete earlier prompts. Notebook revelations require the relevant conversation, rather than merely defeating a villain.
- A villain's dedicated defeat conversation avoids a second unrelated defeat-memory taunt being prepended to it.

## Speech coverage

The old export only selected the first encounter and first hub conversation for most characters. It omitted later story branches, ordinary comms, repeat introductions, results, advice, rerolls and idle conversations. The revised exporter inventories all these surfaces. Unbounded numerical voice templates were replaced with fixed spoken lines; the numbers remain visible in game statistics.

Upgrade comms and results now call voice playback. Stopping speech also cancels pending asynchronous playback so an old line cannot start after its box closes. All active spoken text is checked against the recording manifest and file hashes.

## Music and portraits

The Fortress now uses an original restrained command-centre score with a low pulse and unresolved tension. Global music gain is reduced, the new-player slider default is lower, and speech ducking is applied before fades. Previously the next frame overwrote the ducking target before it affected the mix.

The full-screen “is on comms” intro card now loads the real transparent character artwork, falling back to the character's real portrait when intro art is absent. It no longer substitutes the generic SVG bust.
