# Opening motion comic — production storyboard

Volt narrates the entire opening. Introduce the five heroes, then seven villains, before showing Volt. No partnership/revelation spoilers. All scenes require bespoke action art, distinct physical poses and continuous secondary movement; comms portraits are separate assets. Camera follows the relevant action, settles before speech, and reframes for mobile. Provide subtitles, manual advance, skip, replay and reduced motion.

Volt: male young adult, natural hidden electricity, already helps his neighbourhood; warm and earnest, humour covers nerves. No fame/revenge or invented traumatic origin. Later team relationships continue through the existing mission: registered signatures threatened, Fortress shield safe, unregistered Volt can act, Patch support/extraction, three relays then Gravemark then tower.

## Asset and motion requirements

Each action sequence has four distinct chronological illustrated keyframes at minimum: anticipation, contact, release, aftermath. Maintain setting and identity. Runtime movement must include relevant continuous effects and reactions, not simply pan across the same standing portrait. Action timing precedes the held aftermath; do not endlessly repeat a rescue or impact underneath narration. Review physical continuity before approving scenes.

Fifteen bespoke scene atlases are now implemented: all five heroes, seven villains, Volt's rescue, the broadcast crisis and the Fortress arrival/briefing. Four illustrated key poses per atlas use a moving page camera, timed physical impacts, individually styled animated power effects, weather and debris. Aftermath panels hold for the narration instead of looping the attack. The delivery uses limited motion-comic animation; it does not interpolate every character limb as a conventional animated film would.

Volt narrates all sixteen beats. His established swept black/silver helmet crest, narrow blue visor and angular jaw are identity constraints; the initial rounded neighbourhood helmet was rejected and replaced before integration. Hero comms use separate transparent high-resolution cutouts.

Controls: automatic advance waits for actual voice playback completion; failed audio falls back to reading time. Next scene, Skip and notebook replay remain available. Reduced motion removes shake and animated effects and makes camera changes immediate.
