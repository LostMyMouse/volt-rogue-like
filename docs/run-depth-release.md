# Weapon depth, supply stops and villain arenas

Each equipped style has its own 32-card pool: 16 active hero channels, 12 passive adaptations and four additional weapon techniques. Cards from other weapons are never offered. The underlying hero channels retain their familiar status effects, but the physical delivery differs: close Brawler impact, Arc Caster forks/parallel piercing splinters, Titan delayed ground fractures, Speedster afterimages and Magnetar return wakes/orbit pulses. Dash and Overload channels also use weapon delivery patterns. The twenty new techniques add effects such as steering marked-target bolts, parry counterpunches, lance cooldown recovery, heavy-strike damage reduction, delayed aftershocks, return-disc roots and a disc-catching light burst. They are not twenty renamed stat upgrades.

Hybrid powers are now collected in runs. After the existing later-run unlock, owning a power from both required heroes makes their hybrid eligible in comms offers. The player chooses it instead of a normal card. Multiple hybrids may be acquired, but each remains active only while both heroes are represented in the current build. Nothing is selected in the Fortress; the greenhouse explains recipes. Old saved recipe selections are ignored, and acquired hybrids reset with the run.

Before every villain, a safe supply stop appears between the last ordinary roof and the boss. It does not consume or skip the boss room. Patch is the superhero field supplier, using exactly one already-recorded greeting: “Tech locker's open. Don't break anything.” Her displayed store offers three independently random categories, with duplicates allowed: a weapon-bound boon, a 35-health repair, or 12 permanent Renown shards. Three unusable health offers at full health are prevented. Offer stock stays fixed until leaving and sold items cannot be bought twice.

Temporary field credits start at 25 and come from defeats: 1 per ordinary enemy, 2 per elite and 15 per boss. They expire after the run. Prices are 75 for a boon, 45 for health and 60 for permanent Renown. Purchasing the displayed boon triggers its hero’s real voiced comms; the exact bought boon is then granted, with no second random power draft. No additional shopkeeper recordings were generated.

Seven separately illustrated districts replace the shared roof colour filter: furnace foundry, sonic concert district, corroded botanical block, mirrored glass towers, frozen docks, Gravemark’s stone roof and Redactor’s high-rise control-floor interior. They preserve the established combat footprint, perimeter obstruction locations and open exits. They are distinct theme paintings, not a unique new painting for every individual room. Redactor’s camera frames more of the high windows. A 20-cell transparent hand-inked weapon effect atlas supports the four heroes and five weapon motions. Prompts and final asset paths are in run-depth-art.json.

## Enemy pressure

- Lockjaw: base health 80 → 110, approach speed 105 → 145, initiation range 140 → 185, faster lunge and shorter recovery. Shield-facing hits now deal 50% damage (previously 40%), so they visibly chip health. Rear hits still deal full damage and stagger, but queue a clearly warned turning bash after stagger ends, with a cooldown and shared attack ticket. Its wider delayed sweep remains parryable; two clean parries still break its 70 Composure.
- Relay: base health 42 → 65, protection 20% → 40%, link range 280 → 360, channel 2.8s → 4s and recovery 2.2s → 1.6s. Direct hits, stun, exposure and death still sever links. Protection does not stack and never buffs a boss or another Relay.
- District/Gravemark boss health ×1.6; Redactor ×1.8. Boss Composure 180 → 300, Redactor 360. Windups ×0.85 with a 0.38s floor, moving-ability/projectile speeds ×1.18, recovery ×0.72 with a 0.3s floor, combo pauses ×0.8. Attack footprints, safe gaps and the final 0.3s of frozen aim remain readable. Parries still interrupt and retain their punish window. Accessibility health/speed controls and risk contracts remain separate multipliers.
