# Motion comic and repeat-run expansion

The opening introduces the five heroes and seven villains in bespoke action scenes narrated by Volt, followed by his neighbourhood rescue, the broadcast and Fortress arrival. Use the authoritative Volt concept sheet for his black-and-silver crested helmet. Fifteen four-pose scene atlases have individual moving power effects and a page camera, with readable captions, automatic/manual advance, skip, reduced motion and replay. Six separate transparent high-resolution cutouts replace enlarged comms-card portraits. All active spoken lines use Eleven v4 with a complete source/checksum inventory.

Lockjaw enters ordinary rooftops from district two, roof two. Its frontal shield reduces health damage by 60% while approaching but does not reduce attack pressure on Composure. An 0.8-second bash tell precedes a delayed baton sweep; both are parryable. A parry cancels the combo. Two clean parries break its 70 Composure. Only one Lockjaw can begin a combo at once. Later waves may contain two.

Relay arrives from district three, roof two, at most one per wave. After a 1.1-second signal it channels 20% damage protection to nearby ordinary allies within line of sight. It never buffs itself, another Relay or a villain. A direct hit interrupts it, and stun, exposure or death immediately removes the links. Neither unit adds a boss phase or bar. Each replaces two Rushers in the wave budget. First-meeting comms explain the counters.

Every painted Fortress room has a connected floor path and an interaction. The workshop retains permanent upgrades, the armoury retains styles and adds a variant station, the greenhouse prepares hybrid resonance, the trophy area sets risk contracts, the bunk opens the notebook/replay and the training room opens practice. Furniture and the central command table remain solid.

After a victory, risk contracts offer three handicaps at ranks 0–3: enemy health +15%, incoming damage +12%, or maximum health −8% per rank. Each rank adds 10% Shards, and winning at Risk 3+ adds one Hero Licence. Choices snapshot at deployment, do not change accessibility tuning, and never affect practice.

Each unlocked style has a permanent variant costing two Hero Licences, freely toggled after purchase: Brawler's Circuit Breaker adds a wider third-hit burst; Arc Caster's Split Coil forks every fourth bolt; Titan's Faultline adds a piercing ground arc; Speedster's Afterimage adds a delayed Blitz echo; Magnetar's Return Current empowers and briefly roots on return hits.

Patch's run crates now include Arc Blade (piercing Strike follow-through), Static Well (Special's first target leaves a damage field) and Storm Lance (directional Overload). These are single-acquisition mechanical modifications and work alongside hero slot powers.

After five deployments and a victory, greenhouse recipes can be prepared for the next run. They activate only while the build contains a power from each named hero: Halo/Thorn Sunroot grows a burning root field from Special, Bastion/Echo Mindwall releases a psychic parry counterpulse, and Halo/Echo Prism Current arcs light from marked Strike targets. Replacing the last required power suspends the hybrid.

Validation covers actual damage/guard/parry/channel scenarios, later-wave budgets, frozen contracts/rewards, hybrid prerequisites, modified attacks, connected room access, comic assets/order/camera/controls and desktop/mobile captions. Existing mobile menus, tutorial, audio lifecycle, story gating, music ducking, Composure and locomotion suites also run. Physical iPhone Safari is still untested. Sprite locomotion retains limited authored frames; eight-frame Volt jog registration improves it but is not full skeletal animation.
