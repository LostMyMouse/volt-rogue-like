Rookie run · Rooftop

VOLT

Fifteen rooftops between you and the title of greatest hero. Clear a roof, then leap to the next: each jump point shows what's waiting there. Rushers flash a coral arc before they swing, Needles lock an amber line before they fire, and Gravemark guards rooftops 5, 10 and 15. When its purple ring glows, find the gap.

Attacks marked with a closing white ring can be parried: tap Block as the ring closes to take no damage and leave the attacker Exposed. Attacks with a jagged edge are supers: dodge them, never block.
