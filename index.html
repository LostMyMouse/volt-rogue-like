How to play

VOLT

The Redactor's villains hold the city, district by district. Choose which district to liberate next, leap across its rooftops (each jump point shows what's waiting there), then face its villain. Clear your districts, then take Gravemark's roof. Parry attacks marked with a closing white ring; dodge the ones with a jagged edge.
